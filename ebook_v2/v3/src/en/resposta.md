# The Answer

**It was Sofia.**

If you got it right, notice *why* you got it right: it wasn't one dramatic moment. It was because her signs were small — and they kept repeating. If you pointed at Daniel, you did what the police in Perugia did with Amanda Knox (Chapter 2): you read nervousness as guilt.

Go back with this book's method. The clues were there from the first page of the story.

### SOFIA'S CLUES, EPISODE BY EPISODE

- **Episode 1** — "I stay out of the talking behind backs" and, that same morning, Marco's secret. And the question about the ex, at the very first lunch: looking for open doors (Chapters 1 and 8).
- **Episode 2** — Expansive through the whole meeting; silent, phone in hand, at the exact moment Inês is praised. A deviation from baseline with a topic: Inês's success. And right afterwards she asks for the file (Chapter 2).
- **Episode 3** — The "quick, crooked" smile at seeing Inês happy: contempt. And the seed "he looks like he's had a lot of girlfriends" (Chapter 3).
- **Episode 4** — Repeated touches on the arm; Inês moves away, Sofia leans in again. A boundary test. And Rui leaves as soon as she shows up (Chapter 4).
- **Episode 5** — A question answered with a question, "honestly", a passive voice, a denial without a denial, and the blame turned onto Inês. Five signs in one answer (Chapter 5).
- **Episode 6** — Late-night messages, always about Daniel, and a deleted message (Chapter 6).
- **Episode 7** — The Shadow: eyes go to her when the room laughs, and the boss decides with her behind a closed door (Chapter 7).
- **Episode 8** — Inês is in transition, alone and only weeks into Lisbon: every open door Sofia mapped at that first lunch (Chapter 8).
- **Episode 9** — Every former colleague was "jealous" or "weak". And three left in one year (Chapter 9).
- **Episode 10** — Gifts, "you're the only one who gets me", "I've never had a friend like you". Friendship love bombing (Chapter 10).
- **Episode 11** — "Long-distance friends are like that", "Daniel doesn't know you like I do". Carolina drifts further and further away (Chapter 11).
- **Episode 12** — "I never said that" — and the message is there in writing on Slack (Chapter 12).
- **Episode 13** — Leonor praised in front of Inês; the message to Daniel saying Inês is "stressed" (Chapter 13).
- **Episode 14** — Two days of silence, one day of hugs. Repeated (Chapter 14).
- **Episode 15** — Flowers, tears and "I'm going through a horrible time" on the very day Inês pulls back. And Rui on the stairs, who had seen that scene before (Chapter 15).
- **Episode 16** — Reciprocity ("after everything I did"), urgency ("by six") and Saturday's rooftop (Chapter 16).
- **Episode 17** — "Team effort" in public; "the idea was mine" behind a closed door. And the first version sent through her phone (Chapter 17).
- **Episode 18** — Inês's good news topped by a Sofia story. And the tiredness after every lunch (Chapter 18).
- **Episode 19** — Intense and unpredictable; Inês never knows which Sofia she'll find (Chapter 19).
- **Episode 20** — Closed-door meetings, emails without Inês in copy, and the promotion (Chapter 20).
- **Episodes 21 and 22** — When Inês says no: silence, a midnight message, crying in public. The reaction to the boundary says it all (Chapters 21 and 22).

### DANIEL

Daniel was nervous, turned up late, looked away. He seemed suspicious for half the book. But look at the pattern:

- **The lateness** happened once, with an explanation — noise, not pattern (Chapter 1).
- **The averted gaze** was his baseline, on every topic (Chapter 2).
- **He always answered questions with a straight "no"** and short sentences (Chapter 5).
- **He offered proof** without being asked (Chapter 6).
- **He accepted every one of Inês's "no"s** the first time (Chapters 10 and 22).
- **He remembered the cat from September** (Chapter 19).

Scary signs, in isolation. Good signs, repeated. He was the opposite of Sofia.

### RUI

Rui was the Observer from Chapter 7. He saw it from the first day — "I know it" — and kept quiet for almost a year, like the 69% of Darley and Latané's participants (Chapter 24). When he finally spoke, he asked the right question. It's the last lesson of the story: **seeing isn't enough. Someone has to say it.**

> The most dangerous person in the story was the one Inês described, at the end of her first week, as: "I already have a friend."
