# Introduction — Why No One Shows You Who They Are

Everyone you cross paths with presents a version of themselves. The friendly colleague, the helpful neighbour, the person who texts you at three in the morning saying they've never felt this way.

Most people accept that version without questioning it. Then, one day, the mask slips — and they're left thinking: "How did I not see it?"

You saw it. You just didn't know what you were looking at.

### WHAT THIS GUIDE IS

A practical method, in four parts, built on decades of psychology research — from Paul Ekman to Robert Cialdini, from Joe Navarro to John Gottman:

- **Part I — Read.** How to observe face, body, voice, words, messages and groups. The foundation of everything.
- **Part II — The Predators.** Why they choose you, and the tactics of people who want to use you: love bombing, isolation, gaslighting, triangulation, intermittent reinforcement, the comeback.
- **Part III — Power.** Where manipulation happens: influence, work, family, friendships, interest and betrayal.
- **Part IV — Defence.** What to say, which boundaries to set, how to leave, how to help someone who's trapped, how to train.

### HOW EACH CHAPTER IS BUILT

- **A real case** — a documented story that runs through the chapter.
- **The ideas and the studies that prove them** — what research found, and what it means for you.
- **The signs** — what to look for, concretely.
- **Use this** — one way to protect yourself and one way to help someone else.
- **Today's training** — ten minutes in the real world.
- **An episode** — see below.

### INÊS'S STORY

At the end of each chapter you'll find a short episode of a story. Inês is 27 and has just moved to Lisbon. You'll follow her for a year.

The story is fiction. The cases and studies in the book are real.

I won't explain the episodes to you. You'll read them the way you'd read someone's life: with nice people, ordinary days and details that seem unimportant. At the end of each part there's a page for you to write down what you think. At the end of the book, there's the answer.

Just one rule: **read the episodes with what you learned in the chapter — not with what you feel about the characters.**

### WHAT THIS GUIDE ISN'T

It isn't a lie-detecting machine. There is no gesture, expression or pause that, on its own, proves someone is lying or has bad intentions. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you a trick.

What exists are **patterns**: changes from a person's normal, repeated, tied to specific topics, that make sense in context. That's what you're going to learn to see.

### HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

1. Read Part I first. Everything else depends on it.
2. Do the training. Ten minutes a day is enough.
3. Use what you learn to **protect yourself and decide better** — not to control, humiliate or label others.

Be more alert. Not more suspicious.
